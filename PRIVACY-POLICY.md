# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-21_

This privacy policy describes what information is collected when you use the
Yomitan Frequency Combiner website.

## Data we collect

All dictionary processing happens entirely in your browser. We do not have a
backend that receives or stores the files you upload, the dictionaries you
build, or the decks you select.

The site uses [Umami](https://umami.is/), a privacy-respecting analytics
service, to record aggregate, anonymous usage data. Umami collects:

- Page views (which pages on the site are visited)
- Approximate location (country only), browser, operating system, and device type
- Referrer (which site sent you here, if any)
- Anonymous events for key actions, such as which media or genre dictionaries
  are downloaded and how many items are merged

No cookies are set, no cross-site tracking is performed, and no personally
identifiable information is collected. IP addresses are not stored.

You can read more about Umami's data handling at
<https://umami.is/docs/faq>.

## Third-party downloads

When you download a dictionary, the file is fetched directly from the hosting
provider (currently a Cloudflare R2 bucket or GitHub Releases). Those providers
may log the request as part of their normal operations.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at
